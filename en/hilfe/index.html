How does a skill path work?+

A path shows the levels of a skill. Find your current level and next exercise in the Skills area of the app.

Where can I manage or cancel my subscription?+

On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Apple manages billing and cancellation there.

How do I get help with a purchase?+

Email us a short description of the problem. Never send passwords or full payment details. Apple handles refund requests.

How is my data handled?+

The current Privacy Policy explains what data is processed and which settings you can change. Find it under Legal.

A video or exercise does not load. What can I do?+

Check your internet connection and reopen the app. If it continues, email us the exercise, app version, and iPhone model.
