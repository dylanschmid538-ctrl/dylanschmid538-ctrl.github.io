Building an L-Sit: tension before straight legs

A steady support and active core come first.

Updated November 6, 2026
01

Begin with support

Push down through your shoulders and keep your torso steady. If support collapses quickly, start there rather than forcing straight legs.

02

Lengthen the lever gradually

Lift your knees first, then extend one leg, then both. Choose a level you can control. A short, clean hold is a better starting point than a long hold with poor position.

03

Log your progress

Record the variation and controlled hold time. Ranked shows the next skill level and helps you place your results in context.
