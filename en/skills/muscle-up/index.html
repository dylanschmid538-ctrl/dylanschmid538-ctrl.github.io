Learning a muscle-up: find your next step

The pull, transition, and press are easier to plan when you assess them separately.

Updated November 6, 2026
01

Start with the parts

A muscle-up combines a strong pull above the bar, a controlled transition, and a press. Focus on controlling each part instead of chasing one repetition with momentum.

02

Build each part

Work on clean pull-ups and higher pulls. Practice the transition with suitable assistance. Train bar dips at a range you can control before connecting the movements.

03

Track what improves

Record reps, assistance, and quality. The part that stalls is often your next training step. Ranked helps you follow the relevant skill path.

Your path is not guesswork.

A skill path makes progress tangible: your current level, next exercise, and clear goal. Roko helps you find a starting point.
