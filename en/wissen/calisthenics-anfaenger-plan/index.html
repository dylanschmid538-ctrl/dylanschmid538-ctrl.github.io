TRAINING PLAN / RANKED

A beginner calisthenics workout plan

Start with suitable exercises, realistic training days, and progress you can measure.

Updated November 6, 2026
In short

Begin with a few repeatable sessions. Choose one controlled exercise for each movement pattern and record what you actually did.

01

Choose your weekly rhythm first

Plan two or three sessions that fit your life, allowing time to recover between them. The American College of Sports Medicine recommends training major muscle groups at least twice a week and building up gradually. Use this as a starting point, not a universal prescription.

02

Build a simple session

Choose a push, such as an incline push-up; a pull using a suitable bar or rings; a squat variation; and a controlled core exercise. Start with versions you can perform cleanly. If you have no safe pulling equipment, do not improvise an unsafe anchor at home.

03

Make progress comparable

Log the variation, sets, and reps. Compare the same version across several sessions before increasing difficulty or volume. Ranked shows a skill step suited to your level and keeps your workouts in one history.
