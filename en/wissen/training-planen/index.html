PLANNING / RANKED

Fit calisthenics into your week

A plan only helps if you can repeat it in real life.

Updated November 6, 2026
01

Start with realistic days

Choose training days that fit your actual week. A shorter rhythm you can keep is more useful than a packed calendar you abandon.

02

Give each session a clear goal

Choose the skill or exercise that matters today. Warm-ups and easier variations help you begin with control; your level determines the load.

03

Adjust instead of quitting

When your week changes, move a session or reduce its volume. Watch the trend. Ranked keeps exercises and logs together so your next step remains clear.

Your path is not guesswork.

A skill path makes progress tangible: your current level, next exercise, and clear goal. Roko helps you find a starting point.
