A Thenx alternative for calisthenics skills

A large workout library or a personal path through skill levels and ranks?

Updated November 6, 2026
In short

Thenx promotes a large selection of workouts, programs, and exercises. Ranked focuses on skill levels, workout logs, and visible rank progress in an iPhone app.

Features compared

ThenxRanked
Main focusWorkouts, programs, and exercise librarySkill levels, logs, and ranks
PlatformiOS and Android per provideriPhone
Training accessFree download; premium offeringFree download; training subscription
01

For discovering many workouts

Thenx describes a large collection of calisthenics workouts and programs for different levels. According to its site, the app is available on iOS and Android. That may suit you if discovering new sessions is your priority.

02

For tracking your skill level

Ranked shows your next skill-path step and keeps your logs beside muscle groups and overall rank. That is a different emphasis from a large workout selection. Ranked is available for iPhone; training features require a subscription.

03

Which focus fits your day?

Choose based on what you will actually open: a library of new workouts, or your recorded path toward a skill. Both providers describe guided training. This page does not claim an independent quality winner.

Your path is not guesswork.

A skill path makes progress tangible: your current level, next exercise, and clear goal. Roko helps you find a starting point.
