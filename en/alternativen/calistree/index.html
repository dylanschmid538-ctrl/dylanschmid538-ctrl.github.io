A Calistree alternative: is Ranked for you?

A free exercise library and skill trees, or visible skill levels, ranks, and workout history?

Updated November 6, 2026
In short

Calistree offers a free exercise library and says its app has a generous free plan. Ranked focuses on visible skill levels, muscle groups, and ranks; training features require a subscription.

Features compared

CalistreeRanked
Exercise libraryFree on the websiteSkill paths in the iPhone app
Training directionSkill trees and app training plansLevels, training plan, and ranks
Free entryLibrary and a free app planFree download; training subscription
01

For an open exercise library

Calistree makes its exercise library free to access. Its skill trees link easier and harder exercise variations. If browsing many exercises freely is your priority, that is a real strength of Calistree.

02

For a visible path through ranks

Ranked connects skill levels, a training plan, set logs, and progress to a rank system. That may suit you if you want to see where your own path goes after each workout. It is not a broad free exercise library.

03

Choose by your actual use

Explore Calistree’s free library if you mostly want to look up variations. Consider Ranked if you want your skill stages and workout history in a rank journey. Ranked is currently on iPhone and training features need a subscription; review the terms before buying.

Your path is not guesswork.

A skill path makes progress tangible: your current level, next exercise, and clear goal. Roko helps you find a starting point.
